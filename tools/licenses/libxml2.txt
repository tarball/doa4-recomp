toolchain\bin\lld-link.exe, LLVM 22.1.3's own Windows build, holds libxml2 2.9.12 (for merging manifests), which
LLVM's release build compiles in (llvm/utils/release/build_llvm_release.bat at llvmorg-22.1.3). Its source:
https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/libxml2/-/tree/v2.9.12. Its notices: its Copyright file, and those of hash.c and list.c,
which that file names (the trio files it also names aren't built: LIBXML2_WITH_TRIO is off).
